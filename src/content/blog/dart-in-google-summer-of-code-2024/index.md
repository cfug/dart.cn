The Google Summer of Code (GSoC) program focuses on bringing student developers from around the world into open source software development. Google sponsors students to work with a mentoring open source organization on a 12 week (or longer) programming project during the summer. Over the past 19 years, more than 20,000 contributors have participated in Google Summer of Code.

Google Summer of Code（GSoC）计划致力于把世界各地的学生开发者带入开源软件开发。Google 资助学生在暑期与一个担任指导的开源组织合作，完成一个为期 12 周（或更长）的编程项目。过去 19 年里，已有超过 20,000 名贡献者参加过 Google Summer of Code。

We’re excited to announce that the Dart project will mentor contributors (for the fifth time!) in Google Summer of Code 2024.

我们很高兴地宣布，Dart 项目将在 Google Summer of Code 2024 中（第五次！）担任贡献者的指导组织。

### **Are you interested?**

### **你感兴趣吗？**

To get started now, read [the list of project ideas](https://github.com/dart-lang/sdk/wiki/Dart-GSoC-2024-Project-Ideas) to find a match for your skills and interests. Formal applications must be submitted before April 2. We encourage prospective applicants to submit early drafts and ask for feedback.

现在就开始吧：阅读[项目想法列表](https://github.com/dart-lang/sdk/wiki/Dart-GSoC-2024-Project-Ideas)，找到与你的技能和兴趣匹配的项目。正式申请必须在 4 月 2 日之前提交。我们鼓励有意申请的同学尽早提交草稿并征求反馈。
> *The Dart team expects to have enough mentors to accept only a handful of applications, so also check out [the other mentoring organizations](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2024/organizations).*

> *Dart 团队预计导师数量只够接收少量申请，因此也请看看[其他指导组织](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/programs/2024/organizations)。*

If you have questions specific to Dart and GSoC, ask them on [our dedicated mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/dart-gsoc).

如果你有 Dart 和 GSoC 相关的具体问题，请在[我们的专用邮件列表](https://groups.google.com/g/dart-gsoc)上提问。

To learn more about Google Summer of Code, watch the following video or read [the contributor guide for Google Summer of Code](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/student/).

要进一步了解 Google Summer of Code，请观看下面的视频，或阅读 [Google Summer of Code 贡献者指南](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/student/)。
