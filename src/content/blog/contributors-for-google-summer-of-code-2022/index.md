[Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/) (GSoC) is a global program focused on bringing student developers into open source software development. Students are sponsored by Google to work with a mentoring open source organization on a 12+ week programming project during the summer. Over the past 17 years, more than 18,000 students have participated in Google Summer of Code.

[Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/)（GSoC）是一项全球性计划，致力于把学生开发者带入开源软件开发。Google 资助学生在暑期与一个担任指导的开源组织合作，完成一个为期 12 周以上的编程项目。过去 17 年里，已有超过 18,000 名学生参加过 Google Summer of Code。

We’re excited to announce that Dart is a mentoring organization (for the third time!) in Google Summer of Code 2022. Check out the results posts to learn more about the great projects from [2020](/blog/google-summer-of-code-2020-results) and [2021](/blog/google-summer-of-code-2021-results).

我们很高兴地宣布，Dart 是 Google Summer of Code 2022 的指导组织（第三次了！）。欢迎阅读成果文章，了解 [2020](/blog/google-summer-of-code-2020-results) 和 [2021](/blog/google-summer-of-code-2021-results) 年的优秀项目。

### Are you interested?

### 你感兴趣吗？

Get started now by reading [the list of project ideas](https://github.com/dart-lang/sdk/wiki/Dart-GSoC-2022-Project-Ideas) to find a match for your skills and interests. Formal applications must be submitted before April 19th, but prospective applicants are encouraged to submit early drafts and ask for feedback.

现在就开始吧：阅读[项目想法列表](https://github.com/dart-lang/sdk/wiki/Dart-GSoC-2022-Project-Ideas)，找到与你的技能和兴趣匹配的项目。正式申请必须在 4 月 19 日之前提交，但我们鼓励有意申请的同学尽早提交草稿并征求反馈。
> The Dart team expects to have enough mentors to accept only a handful of applications, so also check out the [other mentoring organizations](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/organizations/).

> Dart 团队预计导师数量只够接收少量申请，因此也请看看[其他指导组织](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/organizations/)。

If you have questions specific to Dart and GSoC, ask them on [our dedicated mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/dart-gsoc).

如果你有 Dart 和 GSoC 相关的具体问题，请在[我们的专用邮件列表](https://groups.google.com/g/dart-gsoc)上提问。

For more information about Google Summer of Code, watch the video below. Or read the [contributor guide for Google Summer of Code](https://google.github.io/gsocguides/student/).
