We expect Dart community members to act professionally and respectfully, and
we expect our social spaces to be safe and dignified environments.

我们希望 Dart 社区成员的言行专业、尊重他人，
也希望我们的社交空间安全、体面。

Specifically:

具体来说：

* Respect people, their identities, their culture, and their work.

  尊重他人、他们的身份、文化和成果。

* Be kind. Be courteous. Be welcoming.

  友善、礼貌、欢迎新人。

* Listen. Consider and acknowledge people's points before responding.

  先倾听。回应之前，先考虑并承认对方的观点。

If any member of the community violates this code of conduct,
the maintainers of the Dart project may take action, removing issues, comments,
and PRs or blocking accounts as deemed appropriate.
We will not tolerate harassment from anyone in the Dart community,
even outside of Dart's public communication channels.

如果有社区成员违反本行为准则，
Dart 项目的维护者可以采取行动，
酌情删除 issue、评论和 PR，或屏蔽账号。
我们不会容忍 Dart 社区中任何人的骚扰，
即使发生在 Dart 的公开交流渠道之外。

If you are subject to or witness unacceptable behavior,
or have any other concerns,
please [email us](mailto:conduct@dartlang.org).

如果你遭受或目睹了不可接受的行为，
或者有其他顾虑，
请 [给我们发邮件](mailto:conduct@dartlang.org)。
